What to get in touch about

We welcome thoughtful feedback on our explainers, suggestions for tools or workflows to investigate, and corrections when something needs a closer look. A little context helps us respond well: include the article or topic, what you noticed, and any relevant links.

Editorial ideas

Tell us what you are trying to understand. Specific questions—how a tool handles a large refactor, where a workflow breaks down, or what review habits make AI-assisted code safer—make excellent starting points.
